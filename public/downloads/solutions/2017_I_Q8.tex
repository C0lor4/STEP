When $\ds n=1,\ a_1^2+2a_1b_1-b_1^2=1+4-4=1$

Assume $\ds a_k^2+2a_kb_k-b_k^2=1$ is true
\begin{align*}
a_{k+1}^2+2a_{k+1}b_{k+1}-b_{k+1}^2
&=(a_k+2b_k)^2+2(a_k+2b_k)(2a_k+5b_k)-(2a_k+5b_k)^2\\
&=a_k^2+2a_kb_k-b_k^2\\
&=1
\end{align*}

By mathematical induction, $\ds a_n^2+2a_nb_n-b_n^2=1$ is true for all $n\geq 1$.

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

\textbf{(i)}

$$b_{n+1}=2a_n+5b_n\geq 5b_n$$

$$b_1=2\implies b_n\geq 2\cdot 5^{n-1}$$
Dividing $(*)$ by $\ds b_n^2\implies c_n^2+2c_n-1=\frac1{b_n^2}$

$$b_n\geq 2\cdot 5^{n-1}\implies \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1{b_n^2}=0$$

$$(c_n+1)^2=2+\frac1{b_n^2}$$

$$c_n+1>0\implies c_n+1=\sqrt{2+\frac1{b_n^2}}$$

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}(c_n+1)
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt{2+\frac1{b_n^2}}
=\sqrt2$$

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}c_n=\sqrt2-1$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

\textbf{(ii)}

$$c_n^2+2c_n-1=\frac1{b_n^2}>0$$

$$(c_n-(\sqrt2-1))(c_n+\sqrt2+1)>0$$

$$c_n+\sqrt2+1>0\implies c_n>\sqrt2-1$$

$$c_n+1>\sqrt2 \implies \frac2{c_n+1}<\frac2{\sqrt2}=\sqrt2$$

$$\therefore\quad \frac2{c_n+1}<\sqrt2<c_n+1$$


$$a_2=1+2(2)=5,\  b_2=2(1)+5(2)=12$$

$$a_3=5+2(12)=29,\  b_3=2(5)+5(12)=70$$

$$\implies c_3=\frac{29}{70}$$

$$\frac2{c_3+1}<\sqrt2<c_3+1$$

$$\frac{140}{99}<\sqrt2<\frac{99}{70}$$
