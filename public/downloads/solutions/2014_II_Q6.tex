$$\sin(\alpha+\beta)-\sin(\alpha-\beta)=2\cos\alpha\sin\beta$$

Let $\ds \alpha=rx,\quad \beta=\frac{x}{2}$

$$\sin\left(r+\frac12\right)x-\sin\left(r-\frac12\right)x=2\sin\frac{x}{2}\cos rx$$

\begin{align*}
2\sin\frac{x}{2}\bigl(\cos x+\cos2x+\cdots+\cos nx\bigr)
&=\sum_{r=1}^{n}\left(\sin\left(r+\frac12\right)x-\sin\left(r-\frac12\right)x\right)\\
&=\sin\left(n+\frac12\right)x-\sin\frac{x}{2}
\end{align*}

$$\therefore \cos x+\cos2x+\cdots+\cos nx=\frac{\sin\left(n+\frac12\right)x-\sin\frac{x}{2}}{2\sin\frac{x}{2}}$$

$$S_2'(x)=\cos x+\cos2x$$

$$S_2'(x)=0\implies \cos x+\cos2x=0$$

$$\cos x+2\cos^2x-1=0$$

$$(2\cos x-1)(\cos x+1)=0\implies x=\frac{\pi}{3},\ \pi$$

\textbf{(ii)}

$$S_n'(x)=\sum_{r=1}^{n}\cos rx$$

$$\frac{\sin\left(n+\frac12\right)x_0-\sin\frac{x_0}{2}}{2\sin\frac{x_0}{2}}=0$$

$$\sin\left(n+\frac12\right)x_0=\sin\frac{x_0}{2}$$

$$\sin nx_0\cos\frac{x_0}{2}+\cos nx_0\sin\frac{x_0}{2}=\sin\frac{x_0}{2}$$

$$\sin nx_0\cos\frac{x_0}{2}=(1-\cos nx_0)\sin\frac{x_0}{2}$$

$$\sin nx_0=(1-\cos nx_0)\tan\frac{x_0}{2}$$

$$S_n(x_0)-S_{n-1}(x_0)=\frac1n\sin nx_0=\frac1n(1-\cos nx_0)\tan\frac{x_0}{2}\ge0$$

$$\therefore S_n(x_0)\ge S_{n-1}(x_0)$$

If $S_n(x)\le0$ for some $0<x<\pi$, let $x_0$ be a minimum point of $S_n(x)$ in $0<x<\pi$.

$$S_n'(x_0)=0 \implies S_n(x_0)\ge S_{n-1}(x_0)>0$$

Contradiction.

$$\therefore S_n(x)>0\qquad (0<x<\pi)$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

\textbf{(iii)}

$$S_1(x)=\sin x>0\qquad (0<x<\pi)$$

$$S_{n-1}(x)>0\implies S_n(x)>0\qquad (0<x<\pi)$$

$$\because S_n(0)=S_n(\pi)=0$$

$$\therefore S_n(x)\ge0\qquad (0\le x\le\pi)$$
