$$\because 1>p>0$$
$$\therefore p=\frac23$$

Guilty-verdict probabilities: $\ds \frac23,\  \frac29,\  \frac49$

Let $G$ be the probability that at least two judges give guilty verdicts.

\begin{align*}
G
&=\frac23\cdot\frac29\cdot\frac59
+\frac23\cdot\frac49\cdot\frac79
+\frac29\cdot\frac49\cdot\frac13
+\frac23\cdot\frac29\cdot\frac49\\
&=\frac{20}{243}+\frac{56}{243}+\frac{8}{243}+\frac{16}{243}\\
&=\frac{100}{243}
\end{align*}

Defendant guilty with probability $t$.

\begin{align*}
P(correct)
&=t\cdot G+(1-t)\cdot(1-G)\\
&=\frac{143-43t}{243}
\end{align*}

$$\because P(correct)=\frac12$$

$$286-86t=243\implies t=\frac12$$
