\[ \begin{aligned} \cos3x&=\cos(2x+x)\\ &=\cos2x\cos x-\sin2x\sin x\\ &=(2\cos^2x-1)\cos x-2\sin^2x\cos x\\ &=\boxed{4\cos^3x-3\cos x} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \sin3x&=\sin(2x+x)\\ &=\sin2x\cos x+\cos2x\sin x\\ &=2\sin x\cos^2x+(1-2\sin^2x)\sin x\\ &=\boxed{3\sin x-4\sin^3x} \end{aligned} \]

(i)

\[ \cos3\alpha=4c^3-3c \]
\[ \begin{aligned} I(\alpha) &=\int_0^\alpha(\sin x+2\sin3x)\,dx\\ &=\left[-\cos x-\frac23\cos3x\right]_0^\alpha\\ &=-\cos\alpha-\frac23\cos3\alpha+\frac53\\ &=-c-\frac23(4c^3-3c)+\frac53\\ &=\boxed{-\frac83c^3+c+\frac53} \end{aligned} \]

When \(\displaystyle c=1, \quad \displaystyle I(\alpha)=-\frac83+1+\frac53=0\)

(ii)

Let \(c=\cos\beta\). Using Eustace's method,

\[ \begin{aligned} I'(\beta) &=\left[\frac72\sin^2x-2\sin^4x\right]_0^\beta\\ &=\frac72\sin^2\beta-2\sin^4\beta\\ &=\frac72(1-c^2)-2(1-c^2)^2\\ &=\frac32+\frac12c^2-2c^4 \end{aligned} \]

When \(c=-\dfrac16\).

\[ I(\beta)=-\frac83\left(-\frac16\right)^3-\frac16+\frac53=\frac{245}{162} \]
\[ I'(\beta)=\frac32+\frac12\left(-\frac16\right)^2-2\left(-\frac16\right)^4=\frac{245}{162} \]
\[ \boxed{I'(\beta)=I(\beta)} \]

When \(\displaystyle \frac32+\frac12c^2-2c^4=-\frac83c^3+c+\frac53\)

\[ (c-1)^2(2c+1)(6c+1)=0 \]
\[ c=1,\qquad c=-\frac12,\qquad c=-\frac16 \]

Since \(c=\cos\alpha\)

\[ \boxed{ \alpha=2k\pi,\qquad \alpha=2k\pi\pm\frac{2\pi}{3},\qquad \alpha=2k\pi\pm\arccos\left(-\frac16\right) } \]
