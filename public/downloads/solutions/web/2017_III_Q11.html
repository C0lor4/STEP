(i) Simultaneous Firing

Consider the entire system (truck, guns, and \(n\) projectiles). Since there are no external horizontal forces, the total momentum \(\vec{P}\) is conserved. Initially, the system is at rest, so \(\vec{P}_{\text{initial}} = 0\)

After all \(n\) guns are fired simultaneously, let the speed of the truck be \(u\). The speed of each projectile relative to the ground is \(v\) in the opposite direction (given the muzzle speed is \(v-V\) and \(V=0\) initially). Thus:

\[ \begin{aligned} 0 &= Mu - nmv \\ \implies u &= \frac{nmv}{M} \end{aligned} \]

The total kinetic energy \(K\) gained by the system is the sum of the kinetic energy of the \(n\) projectiles and the truck:

\[ \begin{aligned} K &= \sum KE_{\text{projectiles}} + KE_{\text{truck}} \\ &= n\left( \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \right) + \frac{1}{2}Mu^2 \\ &= \frac{1}{2}nmv^2 + \frac{1}{2}M\left( \frac{nmv}{M} \right)^2 \\ &= \frac{1}{2}nmv^2 + \frac{1}{2}\frac{n^2m^2v^2}{M} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}nmv^2 \left( 1 + \frac{nm}{M} \right) \quad \blacksquare \end{aligned} \]

(ii) Firing One at a Time

Let \(u_r\) be the speed of the truck after \(r\) guns have been fired. Consider the \(r\)-th firing. Immediately before this shot, the truck (with \(n-r+1\) projectiles) has speed \(u_{r-1}\). The speed of the \(r\)-th projectile relative to the ground is \(-(v - u_{r-1})\) (moving backwards). Immediately after, the truck (with \(n-r\) projectiles) has speed \(u_r\)

By conservation of momentum for this single event:

\[ [M + (n-r+1)m]u_{r-1} = -m(v - u_{r-1}) + [M + (n-r)m]u_r \]

Expanding and rearranging terms:

\[ \begin{aligned} (M + (n-r)m)u_{r-1} + mu_{r-1} &= -mv + mu_{r-1} + (M + (n-r)m)u_r \\ (M + (n-r)m)u_r - (M + (n-r)m)u_{r-1} &= mv \\ u_r - u_{r-1} &= \frac{mv}{M + (n-r)m} \quad (*) \end{aligned} \]

To show \(u_n < u\): Note that \(u_n = \sum_{r=1}^{n} (u_r - u_{r-1})\). From \((*)\), for \(1 \le r < n\), we have:

\[ M + (n-r)m > M \implies \frac{mv}{M + (n-r)m} < \frac{mv}{M} \]

Summing from \(r=1\) to \(n\):

\[ u_n = \sum_{r=1}^{n} \frac{mv}{M + (n-r)m} < \sum_{r=1}^{n} \frac{mv}{M} = \frac{nmv}{M} = u \]

Thus, \(u_n < u\) \(\blacksquare\)

(iii) Kinetic Energy Analysis

Let \(K_r\) be the total kinetic energy after \(r\) shots. The gain in energy during the \(r\)-th shot is:

\[ \begin{aligned} K_r - K_{r-1} &= KE_{\text{projectile}} + \Delta KE_{\text{truck}} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}m(v - u_{r-1})^2 + \frac{1}{2}[M + (n-r)m]u_r^2 - \frac{1}{2}[M + (n-r+1)m]u_{r-1}^2 \end{aligned} \]

Substituting \(u_r = u_{r-1} + \frac{mv}{M_r}\) (where \(M_r = M + (n-r)m\)):

\[ \begin{aligned} K_r - K_{r-1} &= \frac{1}{2}m(v^2 - 2vu_{r-1} + u_{r-1}^2) + \frac{1}{2}M_r \left( u_{r-1} + \frac{mv}{M_r} \right)^2 - \frac{1}{2}(M_r + m)u_{r-1}^2 \\ &= \frac{1}{2}mv^2 - mvu_{r-1} + \frac{1}{2}mu_{r-1}^2 + \frac{1}{2}M_r \left( u_{r-1}^2 + \frac{2mu_{r-1}v}{M_r} + \frac{m^2v^2}{M_r^2} \right) - \frac{1}{2}M_r u_{r-1}^2 - \frac{1}{2}mu_{r-1}^2 \\ &= \frac{1}{2}mv^2 - mvu_{r-1} + mvu_{r-1} + \frac{1}{2}\frac{m^2v^2}{M_r} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}mv \left( \frac{mv}{M_r} \right) \end{aligned} \]

Since \(u_r - u_{r-1} = \frac{mv}{M_r}\), we obtain:

\[ K_r - K_{r-1} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}mv(u_r - u_{r-1}) \]

Summing this expression from \(r=1\) to \(n\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{r=1}^{n} (K_r - K_{r-1}) &= \sum_{r=1}^{n} \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \sum_{r=1}^{n} \frac{1}{2}mv(u_r - u_{r-1}) \\ K_n - K_0 &= \frac{1}{2}nmv^2 + \frac{1}{2}mv(u_n - u_0) \end{aligned} \]

Given \(K_0 = 0\) and \(u_0 = 0\):

\[ K_n = \frac{1}{2}nmv^2 + \frac{1}{2}mvu_n \quad \blacksquare \]

Deduction of \(K_n < K\):

From part (i), \(K = \frac{1}{2}nmv^2 + \frac{1}{2}mv \left( \frac{nmv}{M} \right) = \frac{1}{2}nmv^2 + \frac{1}{2}mvu\). Since we proved in part (ii) that \(u_n < u\), it directly follows that:

\[ \frac{1}{2}nmv^2 + \frac{1}{2}mvu_n < \frac{1}{2}nmv^2 + \frac{1}{2}mvu \implies K_n < K \quad \blacksquare \]
