(i)

\[ \mathrm{Min}(x^2,2x)= \begin{cases} x^2, & \quad x^2\leq2x\\ 2x, & \quad x^2>2x \end{cases} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} &x^2\leq2x\implies x(x-2)\leq0\implies0\leq x\leq2\\ &x^2>2x \;\implies\; x>2 \text{ or } x<0 \end{aligned} \]
\[ \mathrm{Min}(x^2,2x)= \begin{cases} x^2, &\quad 0\leq x\leq2\\ 2x, & \quad x<0\text{ or }x>2 \end{cases} \]
\[ \begin{tikzpicture} \begin{axis}[ axis lines=middle, xmin=-2.8, xmax=5.2, ymin=-5.5, ymax=7, width=11cm, height=8cm, axis line style={->, thick}, xlabel={$x$}, ylabel={$y$}, ylabel style={at={(axis cs:0.12,4.7)}, anchor=south west}, xlabel style={at={(axis description cs:0.98,0.40)}, anchor=west}, ylabel style={at={(axis cs:-0.28,6.7)}, anchor=south}, xtick={2}, ytick={4}, xticklabels={$2$}, yticklabels={$4$}, tick style={draw=none}, enlargelimits=false, clip=false, samples=100 ] \addplot[domain=-2.5:0, thick] {2*x}; \addplot[domain=0:2, thick] {x^2}; \addplot[domain=2:4.1, thick] {2*x}; \addplot[dashed] coordinates {(2,0) (2,4)}; \addplot[dashed] coordinates {(0,4) (2,4)}; \node at (axis cs:0,0) [below left] {$0$}; \node at (axis cs:0.85,2.45) {$y=x^2$}; \node at (axis cs:-1.95,-2.65) {$y=2x$}; \node at (axis cs:3.85,6.45) {$y=2x$}; \end{axis} \end{tikzpicture} \]

(ii)

\[ 2\mathrm{Min}(x^2,2x)=5x-3 \]
\[ \text{If} \ \displaystyle 0\leq x\leq2 \implies 2x^2=5x-3 \]
\[ (2x-3)(x-1)=0 \implies x=\frac32,\; 1 \]
\[ \text{If}\ x<0 \ \text{or}\ x>2 \implies 4x=5x-3 \implies x=3 \]

Hence,    \(\boxed{x=1,\; \frac32,\; 3}\)

(iii)

\[ \mathrm{Min}(x^3,4x)= \begin{cases} x^3, & \quad x^3\leq4x\\ 4x, &\quad x^3>4x \end{cases} \]
\[ x^3\leq4x\implies x(x-2)(x+2)\leq0 \]
\[ x\leq-2\quad\text{or}\quad0\leq x\leq2 \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{Min}(x^3,4x) &= \begin{cases} x^3, & x\leq-2\text{ or }0\leq x\leq2\\ 4x, & -22 \end{cases} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{Min}(x^2,2x) &= \begin{cases} x^2, & 0\leq x\leq2\\ 2x, & x<0\text{ or }x>2 \end{cases} \end{aligned} \]

When \(m=2\)

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{1. }&x\leq-2:\quad x^3+2x=2x \text{ (rejected)}\\ \text{2. }&-22:\quad 4x+2x=2x\text{ (rejected)} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \text{So for }m=2:\ \boxed{x=0,\; 1} \]

When \(m=6\)

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{1. }&x\leq-2:\quad x^3+2x=6x \implies x(x-2)(x+2)=0\implies x=-2\\ \text{2. }&-22:\quad 4x+2x=6x \implies x\in \mathbb{R} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \text{So for }m=6:\ \boxed{-2\leq x\leq0\ \text{or}\ x\geq2} \]

(iv)

\[ y=2\mathrm{Min}(x^2,x^3)-5x \]
\[ y= \begin{cases} 2x^2-5x, & \text{when }x\geq1\\ 2x^3-5x, & \text{when }x<1 \end{cases} \]

For \(x\geq1\)

\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=4x-5=0\implies x=\frac54 \]
\[ y=2x^2-5x=2\cdot\frac{25}{16}-5\cdot\frac54=-\frac{25}{8} \]
\[ \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=4>0\implies \left(\frac54,-\frac{25}{8}\right)\text{ is minimum point} \]

For \(x<1\)

\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=6x^2-5=0\implies x=\pm\sqrt{\frac56} \]
\[ y=\frac{2}{3}\left(\pm\sqrt{\frac56}\right)^3-5\left(\pm\sqrt{\frac56}\right)=\mp\frac{10}{3}\sqrt{\frac56} \]
\[ \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=12x \implies \left(\sqrt{\frac56},-\frac{10}{3}\sqrt{\frac56}\right)\text{ is minimum point} \]
\[ \implies \left(-\sqrt{\frac56},\frac{10}{3}\sqrt{\frac56}\right)\text{ is maximum point} \]
\[ x=1\text{ is the turning point of }\mathrm{Min}(x^2,x^3) \]
\[ \text{Then }x=1\text{ is also the turning point of }y \]
\[ \text{When }x=1,\quad y=-3\implies (1,-3)\text{ is also a turning point} \]

Hence the other three turning points are:

\[ \boxed{ \left(\frac54,-\frac{25}{8}\right),\quad \left(\sqrt{\frac56},-\frac{10}{3}\sqrt{\frac56}\right),\quad \left(-\sqrt{\frac56},\frac{10}{3}\sqrt{\frac56}\right) } \]
