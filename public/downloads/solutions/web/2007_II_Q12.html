(i) Both dice first show a six on throw \(r\):

\[ q^{2r-2}p^2 \]

Exactly one die first shows a six on throw \(s\), and the other first shows a six on throw \(r\):

\[ q^{2s-2}\cdot2pq\cdot q^{r-s-1}\cdot p =2p^2q^{r+s-2} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} P(R=r) &=p^2q^{2r-2}+\sum_{s=1}^{r-1}2p^2q^{r+s-2}\\ &=p^2q^{2r-2} +2p^2q^{r-1}\cdot\frac{1-q^{r-1}}{1-q}\\ &=pq^{r-1}(2-q^{r-1}-q^r) \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} E(R) &=\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}r\cdot pq^{r-1} (2-q^{r-1}-q^r)\\ &=p\left( 2\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}rq^{r-1} -\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}rq^{2r-2} -\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}rq^{2r-1} \right)\\ &=p\left( \frac{2}{(1-q)^2} -\frac{1}{(1-q^2)^2} -\frac{q}{(1-q^2)^2} \right)\\ &=\frac{3-2p}{p(2-p)} \end{aligned} \]

(ii)

\[ m=\frac{3-2p}{p(2-p)} \]
\[ \implies mp^2-(2m+2)p+3=0 \]
\[ p=\frac{m+1\pm\sqrt{m^2-m+1}}{m} \]
\[ \because 0<p\leq1 \]
\[ \therefore p=\frac{m+1-\sqrt{m^2-m+1}}{m} \]
