(ii)

By the normal distribution formula, \(\displaystyle \sqrt{\frac2\pi}e^{-2x^2}\) is the density of \(\displaystyle N\left(0,\frac14\right)\)

\[ \therefore \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\sqrt{\frac2\pi}e^{-2x^2}\,dx=1 \]
\[ \implies \int_0^\infty e^{-2x^2}\,dx=\frac12\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}} \]
\[ \because \int_0^\infty ke^{-2x^2}\,dx=1 \]
\[ \therefore \frac{k}{2}\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}=1\implies k=2\sqrt{\frac2\pi} \]

(iii)

\[ \begin{aligned} E(X) &=\int_0^\infty xke^{-2x^2}\,dx\\ &=2\sqrt{\frac2\pi} \left[-\frac14e^{-2x^2}\right]_0^\infty\\ &=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \int_0^\infty x^2e^{-2x^2}\,dx &=\int_0^\infty x\cdot xe^{-2x^2}\,dx\\ &=\left[-\frac14xe^{-2x^2}\right]_0^\infty+\frac14\int_0^\infty e^{-2x^2}\,dx\\ &=\frac14\cdot \frac12\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}\\ &=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{8\sqrt2} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} E(X^2) &=k\int_0^\infty x^2e^{-2x^2}\,dx\\ &=2\sqrt{\frac2\pi}\cdot \frac{\sqrt\pi}{8\sqrt2}\\ &=\frac14 \end{aligned} \]
\[ \operatorname{Var}(X)=E(X^2)-[E(X)]^2 \]
\[ \operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac14-\frac1{2\pi} \]

(iv)

Let the median be \(m\).

\[ \frac12=\int_0^m \frac4{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-2x^2}\,dx \]

Put \(\displaystyle t=2x\),

\[ dx=\frac12\,dt \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \frac12 &=\frac4{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_0^m e^{-2x^2}\,dx\\ &=\frac4{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_0^{2m} e^{-\frac12t^2}\cdot \frac12\,dt\\ &=2\left(\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_0^{2m}e^{-\frac12t^2}\,dt\right)\\ &=2\left(\Phi(2m)-\frac12\right) \end{aligned} \]
\[ \Phi(2m)=\frac34 \]
\[ 2m=0.6745\implies m=0.337 \quad (3\ s.f.) \]
