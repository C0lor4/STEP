\[y=\frac{x-a}{x-b}e^x\]
\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{dy}{dx} &=e^x\left(\frac{x-a}{x-b}+\frac{a-b}{(x-b)^2}\right)\\ &=\frac{e^x}{(x-b)^2}\bigl((x-a)(x-b)+a-b\bigr)\\ &=\frac{e^x}{(x-b)^2}\bigl(x^2-(a+b)x+ab+a-b\bigr) \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta >0\implies \bigl(a+b\bigr)^2-4(ab+a-b)&>0\\ (a-b)(a-b-4)&>0 \end{aligned} \]
\[a-b<0\quad\text{or}\quad a-b>4\]

(i)

\[ \begin{aligned} x^2-\frac12x-\frac12&=0\\ 2x^2-x-1&=0\\ (2x+1)(x-1)&=0 \end{aligned} \]
\[x=-\frac12,\quad x=1\]

Vertical asymptote: \(\displaystyle x=\frac12\)

\[\therefore -\frac12<\frac12<1\]
\[ \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4} \begin{array}{c!{\vrule width 1.2pt}c|c|c|c|c|c|c} x&-\infty&-\frac12&0&\frac12^-&\frac12^+&1&\infty\\[4pt] \hline \rule[5pt]{0pt}{14pt}y&0&\frac{e^{-1/2}}{2}&0&-\infty&\infty&2e&\infty \end{array} \]

(ii)

\[x^2-\frac92x+\frac92=0\implies (2x-3)(x-3)=0\]
\[x=\frac32,\qquad x=3\]

Vertical asymptote: \(x=0\)

\[ \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4} \begin{array}{c!{\vrule width 1.2pt}c|c|c|c|c|c} x&-\infty&0^-&0^+&\frac32&3&\infty\\ \hline \rule[-4pt]{0pt}{14pt}y&0&\infty&-\infty&-\frac{3}{2}e^{3/2}&-\frac12e^3&\infty \end{array} \]
