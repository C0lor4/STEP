(i)

For \(0<x<b\),

\[ \frac{x\sin b}{b}<\sin x<x \]

Area under chord < Area under curve < Area under tangent

\[ \frac12b\sin b<\int_0^b\sin x\,dx<\frac12b^2 \]
\[ \frac12b\sin b<1-\cos b<\frac12b^2 \]
\[ 1-\frac12b^2<\cos b<1-\frac12b\sin b \]

(ii)

Area under tagent < Area under curve:

\[ \int_0^1a^x\,dx<\frac{a+1}{2} \]
\[ \frac{a-1}{\ln a}<\frac{a+1}{2} \]
\[ \frac{2(a-1)}{a+1}<\ln a \]

Area under tangent < area under curve:

\[ \int_0^1a^x\,dx>\int_0^1(1+x\ln a)\,dx \]
\[ \frac{a-1}{\ln a}>1+\frac12\ln a \]
\[ (\ln a)^2+2\ln a<2a-2 \]
\[ (\ln a+1)^2<2a-1 \]
\[ \ln a<-1+\sqrt{2a-1} \]
