(i)

\[ P_2=\frac12 \]
\[ T_1\text{ sits in }S_1:\ T_3\text{ can sits in }S_3 \]
\[ T_1\text{ sits in }S_2:\ T_2\text{ chooses }S_1\text{ or }S_3 \]
\[ T_1\text{ sits in }S_3:\ T_3\text{ cannot sit in }S_3 \]
\[ P_3=\frac13+\frac13\cdot\frac12=\frac12 \]

(ii)

\[ T_1\text{ sits in }S_1:\quad P(T_n\text{ sits in }S_n)=1 \]
\[ T_1\text{ sits in }S_n:\quad P(T_n\text{ sits in }S_n)=0 \]

If \(T_1\) sits in \(S_k\), where \(\displaystyle 2\leq k\leq n-1\),

If \(T_1\) sits in \(S_k\), where \(\displaystyle 2\leq k\leq n-1\), \(T_2,T_3,\ldots,T_{k-1}\text{ will sit in their own seats}\)

Then \(T_k\) is the first passenger whose seat is not available

There are \(n-k+1\) available seats left: \(\displaystyle S_1,S_{k+1},S_{k+2},\ldots,S_n\)

\[ P(T_n\text{ sits in }S_n\mid T_1\text{ sits in }S_k)=P_{n-k+1} \]

This becomes the same problem with \(n-k+1\) seats.

\[ \begin{aligned} P_n &=\frac1n\cdot 1+\frac1n\sum_{k=2}^{n-1}P_{n-k+1}+\frac1n\cdot 0\\ &=\frac1n\left(1+\sum_{k=2}^{n-1}P_{n-k+1}\right)\\ &=\frac1n\left(1+\sum_{r=2}^{n-1}P_r\right) \end{aligned} \]

(iii)

Assume \(\displaystyle P_k=\frac12\quad (k\geq2)\)

Base case: \(\displaystyle P_2=\frac12\)

\[ P_k=\frac1k\left(1+\sum_{r=2}^{k-1}P_r\right)\implies 1+\sum_{r=2}^{k-1}P_r=kP_k \]
\[ \begin{aligned} P_{k+1} &=\frac1{k+1}\left(1+\sum_{r=2}^{k}P_r\right)\\ &=\frac1{k+1}\left(1+\sum_{r=2}^{k-1}P_r+P_k\right)\\ &=\frac1{k+1}(kP_k+P_k)\\ &=\frac12 \end{aligned} \]

By mathematical induction, \(\displaystyle P_n=\frac12\)

(iv)

\[ Q_2=\frac12 \]

For \(n\geq3\), condition on the seat chosen by \(T_1\).

\[ T_1\text{ sits in }S_1 \text{ or }S_n:\ T_{n-1}\text{ sits in }S_{n-1} \]
\[ T_1\text{ sits in }S_{n-1}:\ T_{n-1}\text{ cannot sit in }S_{n-1} \]

If \(T_1\) sits in \(S_k\), where \(2\leq k\leq n-2\),

\[ T_k\text{ becomes the first displaced passenger} \]
\[ P(T_{n-1}\text{ sits in }S_{n-1}\mid T_1\text{ sits in }S_k)=Q_{n-k+1} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} Q_n &=\frac1n\left(2+\sum_{k=2}^{n-2}Q_{n-k+1}\right)\\ &=\frac1n\left(2+\sum_{r=3}^{n-1}Q_r\right) \end{aligned} \]

Base case: \(\displaystyle Q_3=\frac13+\frac13=\frac23\)

Assume \(\displaystyle Q_3=Q_4=\cdots=Q_{n-1}=\frac23\)

\[ \begin{aligned} Q_n &=\frac1n\left(2+\sum_{r=3}^{n-1}Q_r\right)\\ &=\frac1n\left(2+\frac{2(n-3)}{3}\right)\\ &=\frac23 \end{aligned} \]
\[ \therefore Q_n= \begin{cases} \ds \frac12, & n=2,\\[6pt] \ds \frac23, & n\geq3. \end{cases} \]
