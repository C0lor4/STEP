(i)\(\displaystyle \frac{dy}{dx}=u+x\frac{du}{dx}\)

\[ u+x\frac{du}{dx}=\frac{x}{ux}+\frac{ux}{x}=\frac1u+u \]
\[ \implies u\frac{du}{dx}=\frac1x \]
\[ \frac12u^2=\ln x+C \]

When \(\displaystyle x=1,\ y=2,\ u=2\implies C=4\)

\[ u^2=2\ln x+4 \]
\[ y=ux=x\sqrt{2\ln x+4} \]
\[ \because 2\ln x+4>0 \]
\[ \therefore x>e^{-2} \]

(ii) Let \(\displaystyle y=ux,\ \frac{dy}{dx}=u+x\frac{du}{dx}\)

\[ u+x\frac{du}{dx}=\frac{x}{ux}+\frac{2ux}{x}=\frac1u+2u \]
\[ \frac{u}{1+u^2}\frac{du}{dx}=\frac1x \]
\[ \frac12\ln(1+u^2)=\ln x+C \]
\[ 1+u^2=Cx^2 \]

When \(\displaystyle x=1,\ y=2,\ u=2\implies C=5\)

\[ 1+\frac{y^2}{x^2}=5x^2 \]
\[ y^2=5x^4-x^2=x^2(5x^2-1) \]
\[ y=x\sqrt{5x^2-1},\quad x>\frac1{\sqrt5} \]

(iii) Let \(\displaystyle y=ux^2,\ \frac{dy}{dx}=x^2\frac{du}{dx}+2ux\)

\[ x^2\frac{du}{dx}+2ux=\frac{x^2}{ux^2}+\frac{2ux^2}{x}=\frac1u+2ux \]
\[ u\frac{du}{dx}=\frac1{x^2} \]
\[ \frac12u^2=-\frac1x+C \]

When \(\displaystyle x=1,\ y=2,\ u=2 \implies C=6\)

\[ \frac{y^2}{x^4}=u^2=6-\frac2x \]
\[ y^2=6x^4-2x^3=x^3(6x-2) \]
\[ y=x^{\frac32}\sqrt{6x-2},\quad x>\frac13 \]
