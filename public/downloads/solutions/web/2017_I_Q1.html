(i) \(du=x\cos x\,dx\)

\[ \begin{aligned} \int \frac{x}{x\tan x+1}\,dx &=\int \frac{x\cos x}{x\sin x+\cos x}\,dx\\ &=\int \frac1u\,du\\ &=\ln|x\sin x+\cos x|+C \end{aligned} \]

Let \(u=x\cos x-\sin x,\ du=-x\sin x\,dx\)

\[ \begin{aligned} \int \frac{x}{x\cot x-1}\,dx &=\int \frac{x\sin x}{x\cos x-\sin x}\,dx\\ &=-\int \frac1u\,du\\ &=-\ln|x\cos x-\sin x|+C \end{aligned} \]

(ii) Let \(u=x\sec^2x-\tan x\)

\[ du=\sec^2x+2x\sec^2x\tan x-\sec^2x\,dx =2x\sec^2x\tan x\,dx \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \int \frac{x\sec^2x\tan x}{x\sec^2x-\tan x}\,dx &=\frac12\int \frac1u\,du\\ &=\frac12\ln|x\sec^2x-\tan x|+C \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \int \frac{x\sin x\cos x}{(x-\sin x\cos x)^2}\,dx &=\int \frac{x\sec^2x\tan x}{(x\sec^2x-\tan x)^2}\,dx\\ &=\frac12\int \frac1{u^2}\,du\\ &=-\frac1{2u}+C\\ &=-\frac{1}{2(x\sec^2x-\tan x)}+C \end{aligned} \]
