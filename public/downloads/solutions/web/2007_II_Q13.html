\[ \frac{n-r}{n}=1-\frac rn \]
\[ \because e^{-x}\approx1-x \]
\[ \therefore \frac{n-r}{n}\approx e^{-\frac rn} \]

No shared birthdays among \(k\) guests:

\[ \begin{aligned} P_0 &=1\cdot\frac{364}{365}\cdot\frac{363}{365} \cdots\frac{365-k+1}{365}\\ &=\prod_{r=1}^{k-1}\frac{365-r}{365}\\ &\approx\prod_{r=1}^{k-1}e^{-\frac r{365}}\\ &=e^{-\frac1{365}\sum_{r=1}^{k-1}r}\\ &=e^{-\frac{k(k-1)}{730}} \end{aligned} \]
\[ P(\text{at least two share a birthday}) \approx1-e^{-\frac{k(k-1)}{730}} \]
\[ 1-e^{-\frac{k(k-1)}{730}}\geq\frac12 \]
\[ \frac{k(k-1)}{730}\geq\ln2 \]
\[ k(k-1)\geq730\cdot\frac{253}{365}=506 \]
\[ k^2-k-506\geq0 \implies(k-23)(k+22)\geq0 \]
\[ k\geq23 \]

Let \(h\) be the number of guests.

No guest has the same birthday as the host:

\[ \left(\frac{364}{365}\right)^h =\left(\frac{365-1}{365}\right)^h \approx e^{-\frac h{365}} \]
\[ P(\text{at least one guest matches the host}) \approx1-e^{-\frac h{365}} \]
\[ 1-e^{-\frac h{365}}\geq\frac12 \implies\frac h{365}\geq\ln2 \]
\[ h\geq365\cdot\frac{253}{365}=253 \]
