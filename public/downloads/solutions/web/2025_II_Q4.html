(i) Let \(\displaystyle a=\left\lfloor x\right\rfloor,\ b=x-\left\lfloor x\right\rfloor\)

\[x=a+b,\qquad 0\leq b<1\]
\[ \begin{aligned} \because n\in \mathbb{Z^+}\\ \therefore\left\lfloor x+n\right\rfloor &=\left\lfloor a+b+n\right\rfloor\\ &=a+n\\ &=\left\lfloor x\right\rfloor+n \end{aligned} \]

(ii)(a)

\[ \begin{aligned} f_n\left(x+\frac1n\right) &=\left\lfloor x+\frac1n\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor x+\frac2n\right\rfloor+\cdots+\left\lfloor x+1\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor nx+1\right\rfloor\\ &=\left\lfloor x+\frac1n\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor x+\frac2n\right\rfloor+\cdots+\left\lfloor x+\frac{n-1}{n}\right\rfloor +\left\lfloor x\right\rfloor+1-\left\lfloor nx\right\rfloor-1\\ &=\left\lfloor x\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor x+\frac1n\right\rfloor+\cdots+\left\lfloor x+\frac{n-1}{n}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor nx\right\rfloor\\ &=f_n(x) \end{aligned} \]
\[\boxed{f_n\left(x+\frac1n\right)=f_n(x)}\]

(ii)(b) If \(0\leq t<\dfrac{1}{n}\implies 0\leq t<1\)

For \(0\leq t<\dfrac1n\implies 0\leq nt<1\implies\lfloor nt\rfloor=0\)

For \(\displaystyle r=0,1,\dots,n-1 \implies 0\leq t+\frac rn<\frac1n+\frac rn=\frac{r+1}{n}\leq1\)

\(\displaystyle\because t<\dfrac1n\quad \therefore t+\frac rn<1 \implies \left\lfloor t+\frac rn\right\rfloor=0\)

\[\implies \boxed{f_n(t)=0}\]

(ii)(c) Let \(\displaystyle t=x-\frac{k}{n} \text{ and } 0\leq x-\frac{k}{n}<\frac1n\implies x=t+\dfrac{k}{n}\)

From part(ii)(a)\(\displaystyle \implies f_n(x)=f_n\left(t+\frac{k}{n}\right)=f_n(t)\)

\[\because f_n(t)=0 \therefore \boxed{f_n(x)=0}\]

(iii)(a) When \(n=2\) for part (ii)(c)

\[f_2(u)=\left\lfloor u\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor u+\frac12\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor 2u\right\rfloor=0\]

Let \(\displaystyle u=\dfrac{x}{2} \quad \implies \left\lfloor\frac{x}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\frac{x+1}{2}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor x\right\rfloor=0 \implies \boxed{\left\lfloor\frac{x}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\frac{x+1}{2}\right\rfloor=\left\lfloor x\right\rfloor}\)

(iii)(b) Let

\[S=\left\lfloor\frac{x+1}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\frac{x+2}{2^2}\right\rfloor+\cdots+\left\lfloor\frac{x+2^k}{2^{k+1}}\right\rfloor+\cdots\]

When \(x=\dfrac{u}{2^k}\), from part (iii)(a),

\[\left\lfloor\frac{u}{2^{k+1}}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\frac{u+2^k}{2^{k+1}}\right\rfloor=\left\lfloor\frac{u}{2^k}\right\rfloor \implies \left\lfloor\frac{u+2^k}{2^{k+1}}\right\rfloor=\left\lfloor\frac{u}{2^k}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\frac{u}{2^{k+1}}\right\rfloor\]
\[ \begin{aligned} \therefore S_N &=\sum_{k=0}^{N}\left\lfloor\frac{x+2^k}{2^{k+1}}\right\rfloor\\ &=\sum_{k=0}^{N}\left(\left\lfloor\frac{x}{2^k}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\frac{x}{2^{k+1}}\right\rfloor\right)\\ &=\left\lfloor x\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\frac{x}{2^{N+1}}\right\rfloor \end{aligned} \]

As \(N\to\infty\), \(\implies\left\lfloor\frac{x}{2^{N+1}}\right\rfloor=0\)

\[\implies\boxed{S=\lfloor x\rfloor}\]
