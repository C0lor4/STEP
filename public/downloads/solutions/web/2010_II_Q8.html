Contact points:

\[ e^{-x}=e^{-x}\sin x \]
\[ \sin x=1\implies x_n=\frac{(4n-3)\pi}{2} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} A_n &=\int_{x_n}^{x_{n+1}}e^{-x}(1-\sin x)\,dx\\ &=\left[-e^{-x}+\frac12e^{-x}(\sin x+\cos x)\right]_{x_n}^{x_{n+1}}\\ &=\frac12\left(e^{-\frac{(4n-3)\pi}{2}}-e^{-\frac{(4n+1)\pi}{2}}\right)\\ &=\frac12(e^{2\pi}-1)e^{-(4n+1)\pi/2} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n &=\frac12(e^{2\pi}-1)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}e^{-\frac{(4n+1)\pi}{2}}\\ &=\frac12(e^{2\pi}-1)\cdot \frac{e^{-\frac{5\pi}{2}}}{1-e^{-2\pi}}\\ &=\frac12e^{-\frac{\pi}2} \end{aligned} \]
