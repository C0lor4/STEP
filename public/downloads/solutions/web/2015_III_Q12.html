(i)

\[ G_{R_1}(x)=\frac16(x^0+x+x^2+x^3+x^4+x^5) \]
\[ \begin{aligned} G_{R_2}(x) &=\frac1{36}(x^0+x+x^2+x^3+x^4+x^5)^2\\ &=\frac6{36}(x^0+x+x^2+x^3+x^4+x^5)\\ &=G(x) \end{aligned} \]

The coefficient of \(x^0\) is \(\frac{1}{6}\), which is the probability that \(S_n\) is divisible by \(6\).

(ii)

\[ G_1(x)=\frac16(1+2x+x^2+x^3+x^4) \]
\[ G_1(x)=\frac16(x+y) \]

Modulo \(x^5=1\), we have

\[ xy=x(1+x+x^2+x^3+x^4)=x+x^2+x^3+x^4+x^5=y,\qquad y^2=5y \]

More generally, \(x^my=y\) for all integers \(m\ge1\).

\[ \begin{aligned} G_2(x) &=\frac1{36}(x+y)^2\\ &=\frac1{36}(x^2+2xy+y^2)\\ &=\boxed{\frac1{36}(x^2+7y)} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} G_3(x) &=\frac1{6^3}(x+y)(x^2+7y)\\ &=\frac1{6^3}(x^3+x^2y+7xy+7y^2)\\ &=\frac1{6^3}(x^3+y+7y+35y)\\ &=\frac1{6^3}(x^3+43y) \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} G_4(x) &=\frac1{6^4}(x+y)(x^3+43y)\\ &=\frac1{6^4}(x^4+x^3y+43xy+43y^2)\\ &=\frac1{6^4}(x^4+y+43y+215y)\\ &=\frac1{6^4}(x^4+259y) \end{aligned} \]

From \(G_1(x),G_2(x),G_3(x),G_4(x)\), assume

\[ G_k(x)=\frac1{6^k} \left(x^k+\frac{6^k-1}{5}y\right) \]

Base case \(\displaystyle n=1\): \(\displaystyle \quad G_1(x)=\frac16(x+y)\)

\[ \begin{aligned} G_{k+1}(x) &=\frac1{6^{k+1}} \left(x^k+\frac{6^k-1}{5}y\right)(x+y)\\ &=\frac1{6^{k+1}} \left( x^{k+1} +x^ky +\frac{6^k-1}{5}xy +\frac{6^k-1}{5}y^2 \right)\\ &=\frac1{6^{k+1}} \left( x^{k+1} +\left[ 1+6\left(\frac{6^k-1}{5}\right) \right]y \right)\\ &=\frac1{6^{k+1}} \left( x^{k+1}+\frac{6^{k+1}-1}{5}y \right) \end{aligned} \]

Due to mathematical induction, the statement is true for all \(n\geq1\).

If \(5\nmid n\), then \(x^n\) contributes no \(x^0\) term,

\[ \mathrm{P}(5\mid S_n)=\frac1{6^n}\cdot\frac{6^n-1}{5}=\boxed{\frac15\left(1-\frac1{6^n}\right)} \]

If \(5\mid n\), then \(x^n=x^0\),

\[ \mathrm{P}(5\mid S_n)=\frac1{6^n} \left(1+\frac{6^n-1}{5}\right)=\boxed{\frac15\left(1+\frac4{6^n}\right)} \]
