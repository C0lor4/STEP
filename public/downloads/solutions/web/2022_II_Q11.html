(i) Outcomes:

\[ \text{all }N\text{ good:}\quad \Pr=q^N,\qquad \text{gain}=N-T \]
\[ \text{tested }T\text{ good, }N-T\text{ contains infected:}\quad \Pr=q^T-q^N,\qquad \text{gain}=-D \]
\[ \text{a tested stick infected:}\quad \Pr=1-q^T,\qquad \text{gain}=0 \]
\[ E_{\mathrm{gain}} =(N-T)q^N-D(q^T-q^N) =(N-T+D)q^N-Dq^T \]
\[ E_{\mathrm{loss}}=-E_{\mathrm{gain}} =Dq^T-(N-T+D)q^N \]

(ii)

\[ \alpha=\frac{DT}{N(N-T+D)} =1-\frac{(N-T)(N+D)}{N(N-T+D)} \]
\[ \because 0\leq T<N,\qquad D>0 \]
\[ \therefore 0\leq\alpha<1 \]
\[ \frac{dE}{dq} =N(N-T+D)q^{N-1}-DTq^{T-1} =q^{T-1}\!\Bigl(N(N-T+D)q^{N-T}-DT\Bigr) \]
\[ \frac{dE}{dq}=0 \implies q^{N-T}=\frac{DT}{N(N-T+D)}=\alpha \]
\[ E=(N-T+D)q^T\alpha-Dq^T =q^T\!\Bigl((N-T+D)\alpha-D\Bigr) \]
\[ \because q^T=\alpha^{T/(N-T)} \]
\[ \therefore E_{\min}=-\frac{D(N-T)}{N}\,q^T \]

(iii)

\[ \begin{aligned} E_{\mathrm{loss}}(T+1)-E_{\mathrm{loss}}(T) &=Dq^{T+1}-(N-T-1+D)q^N-Dq^T+(N-T+D)q^N\\ &=Dq^T(q-1)+q^N\\ &=q^N-Dpq^T \end{aligned} \]

It is increasing when \(q^{N-T}>Dp\).

As \(p\to0\):

\[ q^{N-T}=(1-p)^{N-T}\to1,\qquad Dp\to0 \]

\(\exists\,\beta>0\) such that for \(0<p<\beta\), \(q^{N-T}>Dp\) for all \(T\).

\[ E_{\mathrm{loss}}(T+1)-E_{\mathrm{loss}}(T)>0 \qquad \forall\,T \]

\(E_{\mathrm{loss}}(T)\) is increasing, so it is minimized at \(T=0\).
