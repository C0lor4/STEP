\[ E(X)=p+\frac p3+p^2=\frac43 \]
\[ \implies (3p-2)(p+2)=0 \]
\[ \because 1>p>0 \]
\[ \therefore p=\frac23 \]

Guilty-verdict probabilities: \(\displaystyle \frac23,\ \frac29,\ \frac49\).

Let \(G\) be the probability that at least two judges give guilty verdicts.

\[ \begin{aligned} G &=\frac23\cdot\frac29\cdot\frac59 +\frac23\cdot\frac49\cdot\frac79 +\frac29\cdot\frac49\cdot\frac13 +\frac23\cdot\frac29\cdot\frac49\\ &=\frac{20}{243}+\frac{56}{243} +\frac{8}{243}+\frac{16}{243}\\ &=\frac{100}{243} \end{aligned} \]

Defendant guilty with probability \(t\).

\[ \begin{aligned} P(\mathrm{correct}) &=t\cdot G+(1-t)\cdot(1-G)\\ &=\frac{143-43t}{243} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \because P(\mathrm{correct})=\frac12 \]
\[ 286-86t=243\implies t=\frac12 \]
