(i)

\[ x+3\sqrt{x}-\frac12=0 \]
\[ y^2+3y-\frac12=0\implies y=\frac{-3\pm\sqrt{11}}2 \]
\[ \because y\geq 0 \]
\[ \therefore x=y^2=\biggl(\frac{\sqrt{11}-3}{2}\biggr)^2=\frac{10-3\sqrt{11}}2 \]

(ii)(a) Let \(\displaystyle y=\sqrt{x+2},\ y\geq 0\)

\[ x+10\sqrt{x+2}-22=0 \]
\[ y^2-2+10y-22=0 \]
\[ y^2+10y-24=0 \]
\[ (y+12)(y-2)=0 \]
\[ \because y\geq 0 \]
\[ x+2=y=4\implies x=2 \]

(ii)(b) Let \(\displaystyle y=x^2-4x,\ u=\sqrt{2y-3},\ y=\frac{u^2+3}{2}\)

\[ x^2-4x+\sqrt{2x^2-8x-3}-9=0 \]
\[ y+\sqrt{2y-3}-9=0 \]
\[ \frac{u^2+3}{2}+u-9=0 \]
\[ (u+5)(u-3)=0\implies u=3\implies y=6 \]
\[ x^2-4x=6\implies x=2\pm\sqrt{10} \]
