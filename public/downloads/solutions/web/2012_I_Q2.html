(i)

\[ y=x^4-6x^2+9=(x^2-3)^2 \]
\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=4x^3-12x=4x(x^2-3) \]
\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=0\implies x=0,\ \pm\sqrt3 \]
\[ y(0)=9,\qquad y(\pm\sqrt3)=0 \]

Stationary points are

\[ (-\sqrt3,0),\quad (0,9),\quad (\sqrt3,0) \]
\[ x^4-6x^2=(x^2-3)^2-9 \]
\[ x^4-6x^2+b=0\iff \left(x^2-3\right)^2=9-b \]

so \(n\) is the number of points where the sketched curve meets the line \(y=9-b\).

\[ \begin{array}{c||c|c|c|c|c} n & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4\\ \hline b & b>9 & \text{none} & b<0\text{ or }b=9 & b=0 & 0<b<9 \end{array} \]

(ii) Let \(\displaystyle \mathrm{f}_a(x)=x^4-6x^2+ax+b\)

\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=4x^3-12x+a,\qquad \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=12x^2-12 \]
\[ \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=0\implies x=\pm1 \]

When \(\displaystyle x=1,\ 4-12+a=0\implies a=8\)

When \(\displaystyle x=-1, \ -4+12+a=0\implies a=-8\)

When \(a=8\),

\[ y=x^4-6x^2+8x+b,\qquad \frac{dy}{dx}=4(x-1)^2(x+2) \]
\[ \frac{dy}{dx}<0\quad (x<-2),\qquad \frac{dy}{dx}\geq 0\quad(x>-2) \]

Minimum point has \(x=-2,\ y(-2)=b-24\)

\[ \begin{array}{c||c|c|c} b & b>24 & b=24 & b<24\\ \hline \text{roots} & 0 & 1 & 2 \end{array} \]

\(\because\ \displaystyle \mathrm{f}_{-8}(x)=x^4-6x^2-8x+b=\mathrm{f}_{8}(-x)\)

\(\therefore\) The roots count is the same for \(\displaystyle a=-8\)

(iii)

\[ y=x^4-6x^2+ax,\qquad a>8 \]
\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=4x^3-12x+a \]

Let \(\displaystyle \mathrm{g}(x)=4x^3-12x+a\)

\[ \mathrm{g}'(x)=12x^2-12 \]
\[ \mathrm{g}(-1)=a+8>0,\qquad \mathrm{g}(1)=a-8>0 \]
\[ \mathrm{g}(-2)=a-8>0,\qquad \lim_{x\to-\infty}\mathrm{g}(x)=-\infty \]

There is one stationary point at \(x=\alpha\), where \(\alpha<-2\)

\[ 4\alpha^3-12\alpha+a=0 \]
\[ a=12\alpha-4\alpha^3 \]
\[ y(\alpha)=\alpha^4-6\alpha^2+a\alpha =3\alpha^2(2-\alpha^2)<0 \]

\(\displaystyle\frac{dy}{dx}<0\) for \(x<\alpha\) and \(\displaystyle\frac{dy}{dx}>0\) for \(x>\alpha\), so \(y=0\) has exactly two roots.

\[ y=x(x^3-6x+a) \]

Let \(\beta\) be the negative root of \(\displaystyle x^3-6x+a=0\)

\[ \beta<\alpha<-2,\qquad y=0\text{ at }x=\beta,0 \]

\(\displaystyle\mathrm{g}\) has a minimum value \(a-8\) at \(x=1\), so the curve is close to flat near \(x=1\) when \(a\) is close to \(8\).
