(i)

When \(n=1\):

\[ p_1(x)=(x+1)^2-3x=x^2-x+1 \]
\[ \implies (x+1)p_1(x)=(x+1)(x^2-x+1)=x^3+1=(x+1)q_1(x) \]
\[ \implies p_1(x)=q_1(x) \]

When \(n=2\):

\[ p_2(x)=(x+1)^4-5x(x^2+x+1) \]
\[ \implies p_2(x)=(x^4+4x^3+6x^2+4x+1)-(5x^3+5x^2+5x)=x^4-x^3+x^2-x+1 \]
\[ \implies (x+1)p_2(x)=(x+1)(x^4-x^3+x^2-x+1)=x^5+1=(x+1)q_2(x) \]
\[ \implies p_2(x)=q_2(x) \]

When \(n=3\):

\[ p_3(x)=(x+1)^6-7x(x^2+x+1)^2 \]
\[ \implies p_3(x)=(x^6+6x^5+15x^4+20x^3+15x^2+6x+1)-(7x^5+14x^4+21x^3+14x^2+7x) \]
\[ \implies p_3(x)=x^6-x^5+x^4-x^3+x^2-x+1 \]
\[ \implies (x+1)p_3(x)=(x+1)(x^6-x^5+x^4-x^3+x^2-x+1)=x^7+1=(x+1)q_3(x) \]
\[ \implies p_3(x)=q_3(x) \]

When \(n=4\):

\[ p_4(x)=(x+1)^8-9x(x^2+x+1)^3 \]

Let \(x=1\):

\[ p_4(1)=2^8-9\cdot1\cdot3^3=256-243=13 \]
\[ q_4(1)=1^8-1^7+1^6-1^5+1^4-1^3+1^2-1+1=1 \]
\[ \implies p_4(1)\ne q_4(1) \]
\[ \implies p_4(x)\ne q_4(x) \]

(ii)(a)

\[ \begin{aligned} q_1(300)=\frac{300^3+1}{301}&=(301)^2-3(300)\\ &=301^2-30^2\\ &=(301-30)(301+30)\\ &=331\times 271 \end{aligned} \]

(ii)(b)

\[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{7^{49}+1}{7^7+1}=\dfrac{(7^7)^7+1}{7^7+1}&=q_3(7^7)=p_3(7^7)\\ &=(7^7+1)^6-7\cdot7^7(7^{14}+7^7+1)^2\\ &=\Bigl((7^7+1)^3\Bigr)^2-\Bigl(7^4(7^{14}+7^7+1)\Bigr)^2\\ &=\Bigl((7^7+7^0)^3+7^4(7^{14}+7^7+7^0)\Bigr)\Bigl((7^7+1)^3-7^4(7^{14}+7^7+7^0)\Bigr) \end{aligned} \]
