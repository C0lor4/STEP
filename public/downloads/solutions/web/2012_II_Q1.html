\[ \frac1{1-u}=1+u+u^2+u^3+\cdots \]
\[ \frac1{(1-u)^2}=1+2u+3u^2+4u^3+\cdots \]
\[ =\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}(r+1)u^r \]

When \(\displaystyle u=x^6\),

\[ \displaystyle (1-x^6)^{-2}=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}(r+1)x^{6r} \]

(i)

\[ (1-x^3)^{-1}=\sum_{s=0}^{\infty}x^{3s} \]
\[ 6r+3s=24\implies 2r+s=8\qquad (r=0,1,2,3,4) \]
\[ \text{coefficient of }x^{24}=1+2+3+4+5=15 \]

The coefficient of \(x^n\)

\[ \begin{cases} 0, & n=6k+\{1,2,4,5\}\\[6pt] \displaystyle \frac12(k+1)(k+2), & n=6k\\[8pt] \displaystyle \frac12(k+1)(k+2), & n=6k+3 \end{cases} \]

(ii)

\[ (1-x)^{-1}=\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}x^t \]

For \(x^{24}\),

\[ 6r+3s+t=24 \]
\[ s=0,1,\ldots,8-2r,\qquad (r=0,1,2,3,4) \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \text{coefficient} &=\sum_{r=0}^{4}(r+1)(9-2r)\\ &=1(9)+2(7)+3(5)+4(3)+5(1)\\ &=55 \end{aligned} \]

For \(x^{25}\),

\[ 6r+3s+t=25 \]
\[ s=0,1,\ldots,8-2r,\qquad r=0,1,2,3,4 \]
\[ \text{coefficient}=55 \]

For \(x^{66}\),

\[ 6r+3s+t=66 \]
\[ s=0,1,\ldots,22-2r,\qquad r=0,1,\ldots,11 \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \text{coefficient} &=\sum_{r=0}^{11}(r+1)(23-2r)\\ &=\sum_{j=1}^{12}j(25-2j)\\ &=25\sum_{j=1}^{12}j-2\sum_{j=1}^{12}j^2\\ &=25(78)-2(650)\\ &=650 \end{aligned} \]
