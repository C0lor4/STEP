(i)

\[ \mathrm{g}(x)=\ln(x+1)-\ln x-\frac{x+c}{x(x+1)} \]
\[ \frac{d}{dx}\Big(\ln(x+1)-\ln x\Big)=\frac1{x+1}-\frac1x=-\frac1{x(x+1)} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{x+c}{x^2+x}\right) &=\frac{\left(x^2+x\right)-(x+c)(2x+1)}{\left(x^2+x\right)^2}\\ &=\frac{x^2+x-\left(2x^2+x+2cx+c\right)}{x^2(x+1)^2}\\ &=-\frac{x^2+2cx+c}{x^2(x+1)^2} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{g}'(x) &=-\frac1{x(x+1)}+\frac{x^2+2cx+c}{x^2(x+1)^2}\\ &=\frac{-x(x+1)+x^2+2cx+c}{x^2(x+1)^2}\\ &=\frac{(2c-1)x+c}{x^2(x+1)^2} \end{aligned} \]
\[ x>0\implies x^2(x+1)^2>0 \]

(i)(a) For \(\displaystyle c\geq\frac12\)

\[ 2c-1\geq0,\quad c>0\implies (2c-1)x+c>0 \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\mathrm{g}'(x)>0\text{ for all }x>0} \]

(i)(b) For \(\displaystyle 0\leq c<\frac12\)

\[ (2c-1)x+c<0\iff x>\frac{c}{1-2c} \]
\[ \boxed{x>\frac{c}{1-2c}} \]

(ii)

As \(\displaystyle x\to\infty\), \(\displaystyle \ln\left(1+\frac1x\right)\to0\) and \(\displaystyle \frac{x+c}{x(x+1)}\to0 \implies \mathrm{g}(x)\to0\)

(ii)(a) \(\displaystyle c=\frac34\)

\(\because \mathrm{g}\) is increasing on \(x>0\)

\(\therefore \mathrm{g}(x)<0\)

(ii)(b) \(\displaystyle c=\frac14\)

\[ \frac{c}{1-2c}=\frac12\implies \text{maximum point at }x=\frac12 \]
\[ \mathrm{g}\left(\frac12\right)=\ln3-1>0 \]

(iii)

\[ \ln \mathrm{f}(x)=(x+c)\ln\left(1+\frac1x\right) \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Let }\mathrm{g}(x)=\frac{\mathrm{f}'(x)}{\mathrm{f}(x)} &=\ln\left(1+\frac1x\right)+(x+c)\left(-\frac1{x(x+1)}\right)\\ &=\ln\left(1+\frac1x\right)-\frac{x+c}{x(x+1)} \end{aligned} \]

(iii)(a) For \(\displaystyle c\geq\frac12\), from part (ii), \(\mathrm{g}(x)<0\) for all \(x>0\)

\[ \implies \mathrm{f}'(x)<0 \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\mathrm{f}\text{ is decreasing}} \]

(iii)(b) For \(\displaystyle 0<c<\frac12\), let \(\displaystyle a=\frac{c}{1-2c}\)

\(\mathrm{g}\) is continuous and increasing on \((0,a)\), with \(\mathrm{g}(x)\to-\infty\) as \(x\to0\) and \(\mathrm{g}(a)>0\), so \(\mathrm{g}\) has exactly one root \(x_0\in(0,a)\).

\[ \mathrm{g}(x)<0\text{ on }(0,x_0),\qquad \mathrm{g}(x)>0\text{ on }(x_0,\infty) \]
\[ \implies \mathrm{f}'(x)<0\text{ on }(0,x_0),\qquad \mathrm{f}'(x)>0\text{ on }(x_0,\infty) \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\mathrm{f}\text{ has a minimum point at }x=x_0} \]

(iii)(c) For \(c=0\)

\[ \mathrm{g}'(x)=\frac{-x}{x^2(x+1)^2}<0 \]

\(\mathrm{g}\) is decreasing on \(x>0\) with \(\mathrm{g}(x)\to0\), so \(\mathrm{g}(x)>0\) throughout.

\[ \implies \mathrm{f}'(x)>0 \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\mathrm{f}\text{ is increasing}} \]
