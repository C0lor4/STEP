(i)(a) \(x^2+y^2=2ax\)

\[ 2x+2y\frac{dy}{dx}=2a\implies a=x+y\frac{dy}{dx} \]
\[ x^2+y^2=2x\left(x+y\frac{dy}{dx}\right)=2x^2+2xy\frac{dy}{dx} \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{2xy\frac{dy}{dx}=y^2-x^2} \]

\(x^2+y^2=2by\)

\[ 2x+2y\frac{dy}{dx}=2b\frac{dy}{dx}\implies b=y+x\bigg/\frac{dy}{dx} \]
\[ x^2+y^2=2y\left(y+x\bigg/\frac{dy}{dx}\right)=2y^2+\frac{2xy}{\frac{dy}{dx}} \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\left(x^2-y^2\right)\frac{dy}{dx}=2xy} \]

(i)(b) At a common point with \(y\neq x\), \(x,y>0\):

\[ m_1=\frac{y^2-x^2}{2xy},\quad m_2=\frac{2xy}{x^2-y^2} \]
\[ m_1m_2=-1 \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\text{the tangents are perpendicular}} \]

At \((c,c)\): \(a=c\) and \(b=c\), giving circles centre \((c,0)\) and \((0,c)\), both radius \(c\). The radii to \((c,c)\) are vertical and horizontal respectively

\[ \therefore \boxed{\text{tangents: }y=c\text{ and }x=c\text{ --- perpendicular}} \]

(ii) \(y=c\ln x\)

\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac cx,\quad c=\frac{y}{\ln x}\implies \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y}{x\ln x} \]
\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{x\ln x}{y} \]
\[ \int y\,dy=-\int x\ln x\,dx \]
\[ \int x\ln x\,dx=\frac{x^2}{2}\ln x-\int\frac x2\,dx=\frac{x^2}{2}\ln x-\frac{x^2}{4} \]
\[ \frac{y^2}{2}=-\frac{x^2}{2}\ln x+\frac{x^2}{4}+K \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{y^2=\tfrac12x^2-x^2\ln x+C} \]

(iii) \(y^2=4k(x+k)\)

\[ 2y\frac{dy}{dx}=4k\implies k=\tfrac12y\frac{dy}{dx} \]
\[ y^2=4\cdot\tfrac12y\frac{dy}{dx}\left(x+\tfrac12y\frac{dy}{dx}\right)=2xy\frac{dy}{dx}+y^2\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2 \]

Dividing by \(y^2\) and writing \(\displaystyle m=\frac{dy}{dx}\):

\[ m^2+\frac{2x}{y}m-1=0 \]
\[ m_1m_2=-1 \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\text{the tangents to the two curves are perpendicular}} \]
