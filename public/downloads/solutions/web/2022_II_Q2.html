(i)

\[ u_{n+1}=\tfrac12\left(u_{n+2}+u_n\right)\implies 2u_{n+1}=u_{n+2}+u_n \]
\[ \implies u_{n+2}-u_{n+1}=u_{n+1}-u_n \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\text{the difference between successive terms is constant}} \]

Let \(k=u_2-u_1\),

\[ u_n=u_1+(n-1)k \]

which is a polynomial in \(n\) of degree \(1\) if \(k\neq0\) and of degree \(0\) if \(k=0\)

\[ \therefore \boxed{u_n\text{ has degree at most }1} \]

(ii) Substituting \(v_n=t_n+pn^2\) into \(\displaystyle v_{n+1}=\tfrac12\left(v_{n+2}+v_n\right)-p\):

\[ t_{n+1}+p(n+1)^2=\tfrac12\left(t_{n+2}+p(n+2)^2+t_n+pn^2\right)-p \]
\[ \tfrac12\left(p(n+2)^2+pn^2\right)-p=p\left(n^2+2n+2\right)-p=p(n+1)^2 \]

so the terms in \(p\) cancel

\[ \implies t_{n+1}=\tfrac12\left(t_{n+2}+t_n\right) \]

By part (i), \(t_n\) has degree at most \(1\), and \(p\neq0\)

\[ \therefore \boxed{v_n\text{ has degree }2} \]

Write \(v_n=pn^2+qn+r\)

\[ v_1=p+q+r=0,\qquad v_2=4p+2q+r=0 \]
\[ 3p+q=0\implies q=-3p,\qquad r=-p-q=2p \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{v_n=p\left(n^2-3n+2\right)=p(n-1)(n-2)} \]

(iii) Substituting \(w_n=t_n+kn^3\) into \(\displaystyle w_{n+1}=\tfrac12\left(w_{n+2}+w_n\right)-an-b\):

\[ t_{n+1}+k(n+1)^3=\tfrac12\left(t_{n+2}+k(n+2)^3+t_n+kn^3\right)-an-b \]
\[ \tfrac12\left(k(n+2)^3+kn^3\right)=k\left(n^3+3n^2+6n+4\right),\qquad k(n+1)^3=k\left(n^3+3n^2+3n+1\right) \]

so the terms in \(kn^3\) and \(kn^2\) cancel, leaving

\[ t_{n+1}=\tfrac12\left(t_{n+2}+t_n\right)+(3k-a)n+(3k-b) \]

Choosing \(\displaystyle k=\tfrac13a\):

\[ t_{n+1}=\tfrac12\left(t_{n+2}+t_n\right)-(b-a) \]

\(b\neq a\): this is part (ii) with \(p=b-a\), so \(t_n\) has degree \(2\) with leading coefficient \(b-a\)

\(b=a\): this is part (i), so \(t_n\) has degree at most \(1\), and the coefficient of \(n^2\) is \(0=b-a\)

In both cases \(t_n\) has degree at most \(2\) and its coefficient of \(n^2\) is \(b-a\), and \(a\neq0\)

\[ \therefore \boxed{w_n\text{ has degree }3} \]

Write \(\displaystyle w_n=\tfrac13an^3+(b-a)n^2+qn+r\)

\[ w_1=\tfrac13a+(b-a)+q+r=0 \]
\[ w_2=\tfrac83a+4(b-a)+2q+r=0 \]

Subtracting: \(\ \tfrac73a+3(b-a)+q=0\)

\[ q=3a-\tfrac73a-3b=\tfrac23a-3b \]
\[ r=-\tfrac13a-(b-a)-q=\tfrac23a-b-\tfrac23a+3b=2b \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{w_n=\tfrac13an^3+(b-a)n^2+\left(\tfrac23a-3b\right)n+2b} \]

\(\because w_1=w_2=0\)

\[ \therefore w_n=\tfrac13(n-1)(n-2)(an+3b) \]
