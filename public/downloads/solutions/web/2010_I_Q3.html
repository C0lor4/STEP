\[ \begin{aligned} \sin(x+y)-\sin(x-y) &=(\sin x\cos y+\cos x\sin y)-(\sin x\cos y-\cos x\sin y)\\ &=2\cos x\sin y \end{aligned} \]

Let \(\displaystyle x=\frac{A+B}{2},\ y=\frac{A-B}{2} \implies x+y=A,\ x-y=B\).

\[\sin A-\sin B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\]
\[ \begin{aligned} \cos A-\cos B &=\cos\bigl(x+y\bigr)-\cos\bigl(x-y\bigr)\\ &=(\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y)-(\cos x\cos y+\sin x\sin y)\\ &=-2\sin x\sin y \end{aligned} \]
\[\cos A-\cos B=-2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\]

Gradient of \(PQ\):

\[ \begin{aligned} m_{PQ} &=\frac{b\sin q-b\sin p}{a\cos q-a\cos p}\\ &=\frac{b}{a}\cdot \frac{2\cos\frac{p+q}{2}\sin\frac{q-p}{2}} {-2\sin\frac{p+q}{2}\sin\frac{q-p}{2}}\\ &=-\frac ba\cot\frac{p+q}{2} \end{aligned} \]

Gradient of \(SR\):

\[ \begin{aligned} m_{SR} &=\frac{b\sin s-b\sin r}{a\cos s-a\cos r}\\ &=-\frac ba\cot\frac{r+s}{2} \end{aligned} \]
\[PQ\parallel SR\iff m_{PQ}=m_{SR}\]
\[\iff \cot\frac{p+q}{2}=\cot\frac{r+s}{2}\]
\[\iff \frac{r+s}{2}=\frac{p+q}{2}+k\pi,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z\]
\[\iff r+s-p-q=2k\pi\]

Since

\[0\leq p<q<r<s<2\pi\]
\[0<r+s-p-q<4\pi\]
\[\therefore k=1\]
\[\therefore PQ\parallel SR\iff r+s-p-q=2\pi\]
