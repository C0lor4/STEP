(i)

\[ x\leq0:\quad -x-(5-x)+1=-4 \]
\[ 0\leq x\leq5:\quad x-(5-x)+1=2x-4 \]
\[ x\geq5:\quad x-(x-5)+1=6 \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\mathrm{f}(x)=\begin{cases}-4&x\leq0\\ 2x-4&0\leq x\leq5\\ 6&5\leq x\end{cases}} \]

(ii) Let \(\displaystyle \mathrm{g}(x)=\alpha x+\beta|x|+\gamma|x-5|+\delta\)

The gradients on the three pieces are

\[ x<0:\ \alpha-\beta-\gamma=-1,\quad 0<x<5:\ \alpha+\beta-\gamma=3,\quad x>5:\ \alpha+\beta+\gamma=1 \]
\[ \implies \beta=2,\quad \gamma=-1,\quad \alpha=0 \]
\[ \mathrm{g}(0)=0:\ 5\gamma+\delta=0\implies \delta=5 \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\mathrm{g}(x)=2|x|-|x-5|+5} \]

(iii) When \(x\leq0\) or \(x\geq5\), \(x(x-5)\geq0\)

\[ x\leq0:\quad x^2-x+4x+x^2-5x=2x^2-2x \]
\[ 0\leq x\leq5:\quad x^2-x-4x+5x-x^2=0 \]
\[ x\geq5:\quad x^2-x-4x+x^2-5x=2x^2-10x \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\mathrm{h}(x)=\begin{cases}2x(x-1)&x\leq0\\ 0&0\leq x\leq5\\ 2x(x-5)&5\leq x\end{cases}} \]

(iv) As in part (iii), \(\ \left|x(x-5)\right|=x(x-5)\) for \(x\leq0\) and for \(x\geq5\), and \(\ \left|x(x-5)\right|=-x(x-5)\) for \(0\leq x\leq5\). Consider

\[ \mathrm{k}(x)-x^2+\left|x(x-5)\right| \]
\[ x\leq0:\quad 10x-x^2+x(x-5)=5x \]
\[ 0\leq x\leq5:\quad 2x^2-x^2-x(x-5)=5x \]
\[ x\geq5:\quad 50-x^2+x(x-5)=50-5x \]

This is piecewise linear with gradient \(5\) for \(x<5\) and \(-5\) for \(x>5\), so as in part (ii) it has the form \(a+b|x-5|\)

\[ x<5:\ -b=5;\quad x>5:\ b=-5 \]
\[ x=5:\ a=25 \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\mathrm{k}(x)=x^2-\left|x(x-5)\right|+25-5|x-5|} \]

\(x\leq0\): \(\ \left|x(x-5)\right|=x^2-5x\) and \(|x-5|=5-x\)

\[ x^2-\left(x^2-5x\right)+25-5(5-x)=10x\ \checkmark \]

\(0\leq x\leq5\): \(\ \left|x(x-5)\right|=5x-x^2\) and \(|x-5|=5-x\)

\[ x^2-\left(5x-x^2\right)+25-5(5-x)=2x^2\ \checkmark \]

\(x\geq5\): \(\ \left|x(x-5)\right|=x^2-5x\) and \(|x-5|=x-5\)

\[ x^2-\left(x^2-5x\right)+25-5(x-5)=50\ \checkmark \]
