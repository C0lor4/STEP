(i)

\[ a(x+y+z)-(ax+by+cz)=(a-b)y+(a-c)z \]

\(\because a>b\), \(a>c\) and \(y,z\geq0\)

\[ \therefore \boxed{ax+by+cz\leq a(x+y+z)} \]

(ii)(a) \(\Delta\) is the sum of the areas of triangles \(PBC\), \(PCA\), \(PAB\), with bases \(a\), \(b\), \(c\) and heights \(x\), \(y\), \(z\)

\[ \therefore \boxed{\Delta=\tfrac12\left(ax+by+cz\right)} \]

(ii)(b) By part (i),

\[ 2\Delta=ax+by+cz\leq a(x+y+z)\implies x+y+z\geq\frac{2\Delta}{a} \]

Minimum occurs when \(y=z=0\), giving \(ax=2\Delta\)

\[ \therefore \boxed{\min(x+y+z)=\frac{2\Delta}{a},\ \text{ at }x=\frac{2\Delta}{a},\ y=z=0} \]

(iii)(a)

\[ \begin{aligned} (bx-ay)^2+(cy-bz)^2+(az-cx)^2+(ax+by+cz)^2 &=b^2x^2-2abxy+a^2y^2\\ &\quad+c^2y^2-2bcyz+b^2z^2\\ &\quad+a^2z^2-2acxz+c^2x^2\\ &\quad+a^2x^2+b^2y^2+c^2z^2\\ &\quad+2abxy+2bcyz+2acxz \end{aligned} \]
\[ =\left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right)\left(x^2+y^2+z^2\right) \]

(iii)(b) The first three squares are non-negative, so

\[ \left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right)\left(x^2+y^2+z^2\right)\geq(ax+by+cz)^2=4\Delta^2 \]
\[ x^2+y^2+z^2\geq\frac{4\Delta^2}{a^2+b^2+c^2} \]

Minimum occurs when \(bx=ay\), \(cy=bz\), \(az=cx\), i.e.

\[ \frac xa=\frac yb=\frac zc=k,\qquad k\left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right)=2\Delta \]
\[ \therefore \boxed{\min\left(x^2+y^2+z^2\right)=\frac{4\Delta^2}{a^2+b^2+c^2},\ \text{ at }x=\frac{2a\Delta}{a^2+b^2+c^2},\ y=\frac{2b\Delta}{a^2+b^2+c^2},\ z=\frac{2c\Delta}{a^2+b^2+c^2}} \]

(iv) \(c\) is the smallest of \(a\), \(b\), \(c\), so as in part (i)

\[ c(x+y+z)\leq ax+by+cz=2\Delta\implies x+y+z\leq\frac{2\Delta}{c} \]

\(x,y,z\geq0\), so

\[ x^2+y^2+z^2\leq(x+y+z)^2\leq\frac{4\Delta^2}{c^2} \]

Maximum occurs when \(x=y=0\), giving \(cz=2\Delta\)

\[ \therefore \boxed{\max\left(x^2+y^2+z^2\right)=\frac{4\Delta^2}{c^2},\ \text{ at }x=y=0,\ z=\frac{2\Delta}{c}} \]
