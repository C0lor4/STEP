$$3y^2\frac{dy}{dx}=3x^2 \implies \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{x^2}{y^2}$$

At $(-a,b)$, $\ds \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{a^2}{b^2}$

Tangent:  $\ds y-b=\frac{a^2}{b^2}(x+a)\implies b^2y-a^2x=a^3+b^3$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

When $a=1,\ b=2 \implies y^3=x^3+9$

Tangent: $4y-x=9$

$$\implies \left(\frac{x+9}{4}\right)^3=x^3+9$$
$$x^3+27x^2+243x+729=64x^3+576$$
$$\therefore 7x^3-3x^2-27x-17=0$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

$$7x^3-3x^2-27x-17=(x+1)^2(7x-17)$$

When $\ds x=\frac{17}{7}\implies y=\frac{20}{7}$

$$\because y^3=x^3+1^3+2^3$$

$$\therefore \left(\frac{20}{7}\right)^3=\left(\frac{17}{7}\right)^3+1^3+2^3$$
$$\therefore20^3=17^3+7^3+14^3$$

$$\implies \boxed{p=20,\quad q=17,\quad r=7,\quad s=14}$$
