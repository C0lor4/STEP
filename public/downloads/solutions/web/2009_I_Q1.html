(i) \(N=3^2\cdot5^3\)

\[\phi(N)=(2+1)(3+1)=12\]

Excluding \(1\) and \(N\), there are \(12-2=10\) proper factors.

\[(m+1)(n+1)-2=10\implies (m+1)(n+1)=12\]
\[(m+1,n+1)=(1,12),(2,6),(3,4),(4,3),(6,2),(12,1)\]
\[\implies (m,n)=(0,11),(1,5),(2,3),(3,2),(5,1),(11,0)\]

Removing \((2,3)\), \(\boxed{5}\) combinations

(ii) Let \(\phi(N)=428=4\cdot107=2\cdot2\cdot107\)

For the smallest \(N\), put larger exponents on smaller primes.

Possible exponent sets: \(427\quad 213,1\quad 106,3\quad 106,1,1\)

Correspondingly: \(2^{427}\quad 2^{213}3\quad 2^{106}3^3\quad 2^{106}3\cdot5\)

\[\therefore \boxed{N=2^{106}\cdot3\cdot5}\]
