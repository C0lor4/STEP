(i) Let \(\displaystyle f(x)=x-\ln\bigl(1+x\bigr)\)

\[f(0)=0,\qquad f'(x)=1-\frac1{1+x}=\frac{x}{1+x}>0\]
\[\therefore x-\ln\bigl(1+x\bigr)>0 \implies x>\ln\bigl(1+x\bigr)\qquad(x>0)\]

When \(\displaystyle x=\frac1k\)

\[\frac1k>\ln\bigl(1+\frac1k\bigr)=\ln\frac{k+1}{k}=\ln\bigl(k+1\bigr)-\ln\bigl(k\bigr)\]
\[\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac1k>\sum_{k=1}^{n}\bigl(\ln(k+1)-\ln(k)\bigr)=\ln(n+1)\]

(ii) Let \(\displaystyle g(x)=x+\ln\bigl(1-x\bigr)\)

\[g(0)=0,\qquad g'(x)=1-\frac1{1-x}=-\frac{x}{1-x}<0\]
\[\therefore x+\ln(1-x)<0\implies x< -\ln\bigl(1-x\bigr)\qquad(0<x<1)\]

When \(\displaystyle x=\frac1{k^2}\), where \(k\geq2\)

\[\frac1{k^2}<-\ln\bigl(1-\frac1{k^2}\bigr)=\ln(\frac{k^2}{k^2-1})=2\ln k-\ln(k-1)-\ln(k+1)\]
\[\sum_{k=2}^{n}\frac1{k^2}<\sum_{k=2}^{n}\bigl(2\ln k-\ln(k-1)-\ln(k+1)\bigr)\]
\[=\ln2+\ln n-\ln(n+1)\]

When \(n\to\infty\)

\[\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac1{k^2}<\ln2\]
\[\implies \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac1{k^2}=1+\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac1{k^2}<1+\ln2\]
