(i)

\[ X*Y=\lambda\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y} \]
\[ Y*X=\lambda\mathbf{y}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{x} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} X*Y=Y*X &\implies \lambda\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y} =\lambda\mathbf{y}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{x}\\ &\implies (2\lambda-1)(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})=\mathbf{0} \end{aligned} \]

\(\because X,Y\) are distinct \( \therefore \mathbf{x}\neq \mathbf{y}\)

\[ 2\lambda-1=0\implies \lambda=\frac12 \]

\(X*Y\) and \(Y*X\) are distinct unless \(\displaystyle \lambda=\frac12\)

(ii)

\[ \begin{aligned} (X*Y)*Z &=\lambda\bigl(\lambda\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y}\bigr)+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{z}\\ &=\lambda^2\mathbf{x}+\lambda(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{z} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} X*(Y*Z) &=\lambda\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda)\bigl(\lambda\mathbf{y}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{z}\bigr)\\ &=\lambda\mathbf{x}+\lambda(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y}+(1-\lambda)^2\mathbf{z} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} (X*Y)*Z-X*(Y*Z) &=(\lambda^2-\lambda)\mathbf{x}+\bigl((1-\lambda)-(1-\lambda)^2\bigr)\mathbf{z}\\ &=\lambda(1-\lambda)(\mathbf{z}-\mathbf{x}) \end{aligned} \]

They are distinct when \(\lambda\neq 0,\ \lambda\neq 1,\ X\neq Z\)

(iii)

\[ \begin{aligned} (X*Z)*(Y*Z) &=\lambda\bigl(\lambda\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{z}\bigr) +(1-\lambda)\bigl(\lambda\mathbf{y}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{z}\bigr)\\ &=\lambda^2\mathbf{x}+\lambda(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{z}\\ &=(X*Y)*Z \end{aligned} \]
\[ \therefore (X*Y)*Z=(X*Z)*(Y*Z) \]
\[ \begin{aligned} (X*Y)*(X*Z) &=\lambda\bigl(\lambda\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y}\bigr) +(1-\lambda)\bigl(\lambda\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{z}\bigr)\\ &=\lambda\mathbf{x}+\lambda(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y}+(1-\lambda)^2\mathbf{z}\\ &=X*(Y*Z) \end{aligned} \]
\[ \therefore X*(Y*Z)=(X*Y)*(X*Z) \]

(iv) Let \(\mathbf{p}_n\) be the position vector of \(P_n\). \(\displaystyle \mathbf{p}_1=\lambda\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y}\)

Assume \(\displaystyle \mathbf{p}_k=\lambda^k\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda^k)\mathbf{y}\) is true

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathbf{p}_{k+1} &=\lambda\mathbf{p}_k+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y}\\ &=\lambda\bigl(\lambda^k\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda^k)\mathbf{y}\bigr)+(1-\lambda)\mathbf{y}\\ &=\lambda^{k+1}\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda^{k+1})\mathbf{y} \end{aligned} \]

By mathematical induction, \(\mathbf{p}_n=\lambda^n\mathbf{x}+(1-\lambda^n)\mathbf{y}\) is true
