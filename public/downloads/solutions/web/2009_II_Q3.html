\[ \begin{aligned} \tan\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\frac{x}{2}\right) &=\frac{1-\tan\frac{x}{2}}{1+\tan\frac{x}{2}}\\ &=\frac{1-\sin x}{\cos x}\\ &=\frac1{\cos x}-\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}\\ &=\sec x-\tan x \end{aligned} \]

(i) Using \((*)\) with \(\displaystyle x=\frac{\pi}{4}\),

\[ \tan\frac{\pi}{8}=\sec\frac{\pi}{4}-\tan\frac{\pi}{4}=\sqrt2-1 \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \tan\frac{11\pi}{24} &=\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{3}+\frac{\pi}{8}\right)\\ &=\frac{\sqrt3+\sqrt2-1}{1-\sqrt3(\sqrt2-1)}\\ &=\frac{\sqrt3+\sqrt2-1}{\sqrt3-\sqrt6+1} \end{aligned} \]

(ii)

\[ (\sqrt3-\sqrt6+1)(2+\sqrt2+\sqrt3+\sqrt6)=\sqrt3+\sqrt2-1 \]
\[ \therefore \frac{\sqrt3+\sqrt2-1}{\sqrt3-\sqrt6+1}=2+\sqrt2+\sqrt3+\sqrt6 \]

(iii)

Using \((*)\) with \(\displaystyle x=\frac{11\pi}{24}\),

\[ \tan\frac\pi{48}=\sec\frac{11\pi}{24}-\tan\frac{11\pi}{24} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \sec\frac{11\pi}{24} &=\sqrt{1+\tan^2\frac{11\pi}{24}}\\ &=\sqrt{1+(2+\sqrt2+\sqrt3+\sqrt6)^2}\\ &=\sqrt{16+10\sqrt2+8\sqrt3+6\sqrt6} \end{aligned} \]

From part (ii), \(\displaystyle \tan\frac{11\pi}{24}=2+\sqrt2+\sqrt3+\sqrt6\)

\[ \therefore \tan\frac\pi{48} =\sqrt{16+10\sqrt2+8\sqrt3+6\sqrt6}-2-\sqrt2-\sqrt3-\sqrt6 \]
