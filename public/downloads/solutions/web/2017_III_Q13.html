\[ \begin{aligned} V(x)&=E\big((X-x)^2\big)\\ &=E\big(((X-\mu)+(\mu-x))^2\big)\\ &=E\big((X-\mu)^2\big)+2(\mu-x)E(X-\mu)+(\mu-x)^2\\ &=\sigma^2+0+(\mu-x)^2\\ &=\sigma^2+(x-\mu)^2 \end{aligned} \]
\[ E(Y)=E\big(\sigma^2+(X-\mu)^2\big)=\sigma^2+E\big((X-\mu)^2\big)=\sigma^2+\sigma^2 \]
\[ \implies \boxed{E(Y)=2\sigma^2} \]
\[ \because \text{X is uniformly distributed on the interval } 0\leq X\leq 1 \]
\[ \therefore \mu_X=\frac12,\qquad \sigma_X^2=\frac1{12} \]
\[ V(x)=\sigma^2+(x-\mu)^2=\frac1{12}+\left(x-\frac12\right)^2=\boxed{x^2-x+\frac13} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} F_Y(y)&=P(Y\le y)\\ &=P\left(\left(X-\frac12\right)^2+\frac1{12}\le y\right)\\ &=P\left(\frac12-\sqrt{y-\frac1{12}}\le X\le \frac12+\sqrt{y-\frac1{12}}\right) \end{aligned} \]

Since \(0\le X\le 1\),

\[ 0\le \left(X-\frac12\right)^2\le \frac14 \qquad\implies\qquad \frac1{12}\le Y\le \frac13 \]
\[ F_X(x)= \begin{cases} 0 & x\le 0\\ x & 0<x<1\\ 1 & x\ge 1 \end{cases} \qquad\implies\qquad F_Y(y)= \begin{cases} 0 & y\le \frac1{12}\\ \sqrt{4y-\frac13} & \frac1{12}<y<\frac13\\ 1 & y\ge \frac13 \end{cases} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} F_Y(y) &=F_X\left(\frac12+\sqrt{y-\frac1{12}}\right)-F_X\left(\frac12-\sqrt{y-\frac1{12}}\right)\\ &=\left(\frac12+\sqrt{y-\frac1{12}}\right)-\left(\frac12-\sqrt{y-\frac1{12}}\right)\\ &=2\sqrt{y-\frac1{12}}\\ &=\sqrt{4y-\frac13} \end{aligned} \qquad \left(\frac1{12}\le y\le \frac13\right) \]
\[ f_Y(y)=\frac{d}{dy}F_Y(y)=\frac{2}{\sqrt{4y-\frac13}} \qquad \left(\frac1{12}<y<\frac13\right) \]
\[ \implies \boxed{ f_Y(y)= \begin{cases} 0 & \text{otherwise}\\ \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{4y-\frac13}} & \frac1{12}<y<\frac13 \end{cases}} \]
\[ \begin{aligned}[t] E(Y)&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} y\,f_Y(y)\,\mathrm{d}y\\ &=\int_{1/12}^{1/3} 2y\left(4y-\frac13\right)^{-1/2}\,\mathrm{d}y\\ &=\left[y\left(4y-\frac13\right)^{1/2}-\frac16\left(4y-\frac13\right)^{3/2}\right]_{1/12}^{1/3}\\ &=\frac16 \end{aligned} \]

From \((*)\), \(\displaystyle E(Y)=2\sigma^2=2\cdot \frac1{12}=\frac16\).
