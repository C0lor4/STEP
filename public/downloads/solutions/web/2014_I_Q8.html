(i)

For \(L_a\),

\[ y-0=\frac{(1-a)-0}{0-a}(x-a) \]
\[ y=-\frac{1-a}{a}x+1-a \]

For \(L_b\),

\[ y=-\frac{1-b}{b}x+1-b \]
\[ y=-\frac{1-a}{a}x+1-a=-\frac{1-b}{b}x+1-b \]
\[ \frac{b-a}{ab}x=b-a \]

\(\displaystyle \because a\neq b,\quad \therefore x=ab\)

\[ y=(1-a)(1-b) \]

Points of intersection: \(\displaystyle (ab,\ (1-a)(1-b))\)

(ii)

\[ b\to a\implies (ab,\ (1-a)(1-b))\to (a^2,\ (1-a)^2) \]

Let \(\displaystyle x=a^2\quad (0<a<1)\),

\[ y=(1-a)^2=(1-\sqrt{x})^2 \]

(iii)

\[ C:\ y=(1-\sqrt{x})^2=1-2\sqrt{x}+x \]
\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=1-\frac1{\sqrt{x}}=1-\frac1a=-\frac{1-a}{a} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} y-(1-a)^2&=-\frac{1-a}{a}(x-a^2)\\ y&=-\frac{1-a}{a}x+1-a \end{aligned} \]
\[ \therefore L_a \text{ is tangent to } C \text{ at } (a^2,(1-a)^2) \]
