If $\ds y=e^x,\ \frac{dy}{dx}=e^x,\  \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=e^x$

$$(x-1)e^x-xe^x+e^x=0$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

Let $\ds y=ue^x$

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=e^x\left(u+\frac{du}{dx}\right)$$

$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=e^x\left(u+2\frac{du}{dx}+\frac{d^2u}{dx^2}\right)$$

\begin{align*}
0&=(x-1)e^x\left(u+2\frac{du}{dx}+\frac{d^2u}{dx^2}\right)
-xe^x\left(u+\frac{du}{dx}\right)+ue^x\\
&=e^x\left((x-1)\frac{d^2u}{dx^2}+(x-2)\frac{du}{dx}\right)
\end{align*}
$$\because e^x>0$$
$$\therefore (x-1)\frac{d^2u}{dx^2}+(x-2)\frac{du}{dx}=0$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

Let $\ds \frac{du}{dx}=v$

$$(x-1)\frac{dv}{dx}+(x-2)v=0$$

$$\frac1v\frac{dv}{dx}=-\frac{x-2}{x-1}=-1+\frac1{x-1}$$

$$\ln v=-x+\ln|x-1|+C$$

$$v=A(x-1)e^{-x}$$

$$\frac{du}{dx}=A(x-1)e^{-x}$$

$$u=-Axe^{-x}+B$$

$$\implies y=ue^x=-Ax+Be^x=Ax+Be^x$$
