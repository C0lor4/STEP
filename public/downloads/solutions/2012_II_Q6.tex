\begin{align*}
BD^2&=a^2+d^2-2ad\cos\theta\\
BD^2&=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos(180^\circ-\theta)
\end{align*}


$$a^2+d^2-2ad\cos\theta=b^2+c^2+2bc\cos\theta$$

$$\boxed{\cos\theta=\frac{a^2+d^2-b^2-c^2}{2(ad+bc)}}$$

\begin{align*}
Q_1&=\frac12ad\sin\theta\\
Q_2&=\frac12bc\sin(180^\circ-\theta)
\end{align*}

$$Q=Q_1+Q_2=\frac12(ad+bc)\sin\theta$$

$$\boxed{\sin\theta=\frac{2Q}{ad+bc}}$$

$$\because\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$$

$$\therefore \left(\frac{2Q}{ad+bc}\right)^2+\left(\frac{a^2+d^2-b^2-c^2}{2(ad+bc)}\right)^2=1$$

$$\implies \boxed{16Q^2=4(ad+bc)^2-(a^2+d^2-b^2-c^2)^2}$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

\begin{align*}
16Q^2
&=\left(2(ad+bc)+a^2+d^2-b^2-c^2\right)\left(2(ad+bc)-a^2-d^2+b^2+c^2\right)\\
&=\left((a+d)^2-(b-c)^2\right)\left((b+c)^2-(a-d)^2\right)\\
&=(a+d-b+c)(a+d+b-c)(b+c-a+d)(b+c+a-d)\\
&=16(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)(s-d)
\end{align*}
$$\boxed{Q^2=(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)(s-d)}$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

For a triangle with sides $\ds a,b,c$, let $\ds d=0$

$$Q^2=s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)$$

$$Q=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$$
