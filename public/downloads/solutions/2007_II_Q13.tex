$$\frac{n-r}{n}=1-\frac r n$$

$$\because e^{-x}\approx 1-x$$

$$\therefore \frac{n-r}{n}\approx e^{-\frac rn}$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

No shared birthdays among $k$ guests:

\begin{align*}
P_0
&=1\cdot\frac{364}{365}\cdot\frac{363}{365}\cdots\frac{365-k+1}{365}\\
&=\prod_{r=1}^{k-1}\frac{365-r}{365}\\
&\approx \prod_{r=1}^{k-1}e^{-\frac r{365}}\\
&=e^{-\frac1{365}\sum_{r=1}^{k-1}r}\\
&=e^{-\frac{k(k-1)}{730}}
\end{align*}

$$P(\text{at least two share a birthday})
\approx 1-e^{-\frac{k(k-1)}{730}}$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

$$1-e^{-\frac{k(k-1)}{730}}\geq\frac12$$

$$\frac{k(k-1)}{730}\geq \ln2$$

$$k(k-1)\geq 730\cdot\frac{253}{365}=506$$

$$k^2-k-506\geq 0\implies (k-23)(k+22)\geq 0$$

$$k\geq23$$

\bigskip\hrule\bigskip

Let $h$ be the number of guests

No guest has the same birthday as the host: 
$$\left(\frac{364}{365}\right)^h=\left(\frac{365-1}{365}\right)^h\approx e^{-\frac h{365}}$$

$$P(\text{at least one guest matches the host})\approx 1-e^{-\frac h{365}}$$

$$1-e^{-\frac h{365}}\geq\frac12\implies \frac h{365}\geq \ln2$$

$$h\geq365\cdot\frac{253}{365}=253$$
