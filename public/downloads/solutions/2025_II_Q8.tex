\textbf{(i)} Total sum $=36$, total sum of squares $=204$.$\implies$Each subset's sum $=18$, each subset's sum of squares $=102$.

$$\boxed{A=\{1,4,6,7\},\qquad B=\{2,3,5,8\}.}$$

\textbf{(ii)}

$$\text{LHS} = \sum a_k^3 + \sum\left(c^3+3c^2b_k+3cb_k^2+b_k^3\right)
= \sum a_k^3+\sum b_k^3 + mc^3 + 3c^2\sum b_k + 3c\sum b_k^2$$


$$\because \ds\sum a_k=\sum b_k \text{ and } \ds\sum a_k^2=\sum b_k^2$$

\begin{align*}
\therefore \text{LHS}&=\ds\sum a_k^3+\sum b_k^3 + mc^3 + 3c^2\sum a_k + 3c\sum a_k^2\\
&= \sum_{k=1}^{m} b_k^3 + \sum_{k=1}^{m} (c+a_k)^3
\end{align*}


\bigskip\hrule\bigskip
\textbf{(iii)} From part (i), let $\ds A'=\{1,4,6,7\},\quad B'=\{2,3,5,8\}$

Then $\ds\sum a'_k=\sum b'_k$ and $\ds\sum {a'_k}^2=\sum {b'_k}^2$.

When $c=8$
$$\boxed{A=\{1,4,6,7,10,11,13,16\},\qquad B=\{2,3,5,8,9,12,14,15\}.}$$

$$\sum_{x\in A}x=\sum a_k+\sum(8+b_k),\qquad \sum_{x\in B}x=\sum b_k+\sum(8+a_k),$$
$$\because \ds\sum a'_k=\sum b'_k,\ \sum a_k=\sum b_k$$


$$\therefore \sum a_k^2+\sum(c+b_k)^2=\sum a_k^2+\sum(c^2+2cb_k+b_k^2)$$
$$=\sum a_k^2+mc^2+2c\sum b_k+\sum b_k^2$$
$$=\sum b_k^2+mc^2+2c\sum a_k+\sum a_k^2$$
$$=\sum b_k^2+\sum(c+a_k)^2$$

So, the sum of squares of A and B are equal.

By part (ii), $\ds\sum a_k^3+\sum(8+b_k)^3=\sum b_k^3+\sum(8+a_k)^3$

Thus, the sum of cubes of A and B are equal.
